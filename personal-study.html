Quick Tools

📜 Read the Gospels in Chronological Order

Follow the life and ministry of Jesus as one continuous reading journey. Parallel accounts are grouped together, so you can read Matthew, Mark, Luke, and John at the same point in the chronology instead of jumping around on your own.

Choose an event

Choose an event from the chronological list.
Chronological harmonies can differ on the placement of a few events. This reading sequence broadly follows the classic public-domain harmony by A. T. Robertson while keeping the interface simple for Bible reading. View the public-domain source.

🗺 Interactive Bible Maps

Explore biblical places on a real interactive map. The base map is OpenStreetMap; BibleMastery adds markers for important Gospel locations. Ancient-site identifications that are debated are labeled approximate or traditional.

Locations are shown on a modern map for geographic orientation. A marker does not claim that every archaeological identification is certain.
